Questions

Is CV Maker free?

Yes. You can create, edit and download as many resumes as you like.

Where is my data stored?

Your account and resumes are saved in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Can I keep more than one resume?

Yes. Many people keep a version for each type of role they apply for. Duplicate an existing resume to get started quickly.

How do I download my resume?

Click “Download PDF” in the editor and choose “Save as PDF” in the print dialog.
