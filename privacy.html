Overview

This policy explains what information HResume handles when you use the website and how it is used. We collect only what is needed to let you build and download your resume.

Data we store

When you use HResume, the following information is stored:

  • Account details: your name, email address and a hashed version of your password.
  • Resume content: everything you enter in the editor, such as your contact details, work history, education and skills.
  • Preferences: the template and color you choose for each resume.

We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell your information.

Where your data is kept

Your account and resumes are saved in your browser’s local storage on the device you are using. They are not uploaded to our servers. This means:

  • Your resumes are only available in the browser and on the device where you created them.
  • Clearing your browser data will permanently delete your account and resumes.
  • Anyone with access to your device and browser profile may be able to see your resumes.

Third-party services

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser requests these files from Google, which receives your IP address as part of that request. See Google’s privacy policy for how it handles this information.

Contact form

If you contact us, we receive the name, email address and message you provide. We use this only to respond to you and keep it only as long as needed to handle your request.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete any resume at any time from your dashboard.
  • You can permanently delete your account and all of its resumes from the Account page.
  • You can ask us about any message you have sent us, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us through the contact page or at hello@hresume.app.
